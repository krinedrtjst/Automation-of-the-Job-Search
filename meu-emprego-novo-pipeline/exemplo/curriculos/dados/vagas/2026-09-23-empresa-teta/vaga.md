# job_desc.md (Fictional Example)

**Role:** Junior Data Scientist (NLP & AI Safety)
**Company:** Company Theta (Fictional)
**Location:** Remote, Brazil (Global Team)

## About the Role
Company Theta is building next-generation, compliant Generative AI assistants for the healthcare and finance sectors. We are looking for a Data Scientist who possesses both rigorous technical ML expertise and a deep understanding of human behavior to help us evaluate, fine-tune, and align our language models. You will be responsible for processing massive datasets, mitigating model bias, and delivering actionable clinical and business intelligence.

## Key Responsibilities
- Architect and maintain robust ETL pipelines to process large-scale multimodal datasets (10TB+) for model training.
- Fine-tune and deploy NLP models (PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers) with a strict focus on fairness, safety, and bias reduction.
- Develop and automate data extraction routines and operational dashboards for clinical stakeholders and business intelligence teams.
- Lead AI alignment initiatives, evaluating LLM outputs against strict Trust & Safety rubrics.

## Mandatory Requirements
- **Tech Stack:** Advanced proficiency in Python (Pandas, PyTorch, LangChain) and complex SQL for data manipulation.
- **Big Data:** Hands-on experience building and managing scalable data pipelines (e.g., PySpark, AWS).
- **Machine Learning:** Proven track record in NLP model evaluation, fine-tuning, and deployment.
- **Reporting:** Proven ability to translate complex epidemiological, clinical, or financial data into actionable automated reports.

## Desirable Qualifications
- **Interdisciplinary Background:** Formal education or strong domain expertise in Psychology, Neuroscience, or Behavioral Sciences is highly valued for our human-in-the-loop alignment tasks.
- **AI Safety:** Experience with RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback), prompt engineering, or adversarial red-teaming.
- **Data Visualization & Legacy Systems:** Familiarity with Power BI, Tableau, and Excel VBA/Macros to bridge legacy clinical systems with modern AI architectures.
